# Task 3 – Digital Literacy Awareness Survey

## Overview
Created a **Digital Literacy Awareness Survey** using Google Forms to explore students'
digital awareness, online safety habits, and cybersecurity practices.

## Form Link
🔗 [Click here to access the survey](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSedd6b22VRS84yHk5bRMWMNYInn4u8uR0TUKrhFMWdo0PjwdA/viewform?usp=header)

## Form Details
- **Title:** Digital Literacy Awareness Survey
- **Description:** Explores digital awareness, habits, and online safety practices among students.

## Questions Included
1. **Name** – Short answer
2. **Registration Number** – Short answer
3. **Email ID** – Short answer
4. **Suspicious Link Behavior** – MCQ (e.g., ignore, verify sender, click out of curiosity)
5. **Confidence in Identifying Scams** – Rating scale (1–5)
6. **Online Safety Actions** – Checkboxes (2FA, password habits, avoiding unknown links, etc.)
7. **Personal Safety Experience** – Short answer (open-ended)
8. **Data Value Misconception** – True/False

## Response Summary (5 Responses)
- 4/5 correctly identified the right action for suspicious links (**verify sender before clicking**)
- Most rated their scam-detection confidence at **3–4 out of 5**
- 4/5 enable **two-factor authentication** and **avoid unknown links**
- Only 2/5 correctly answered the data value question (frequently missed)
