## 🛡️ Case Study: UPI / Online Payment Fraud

UPI (Unified Payments Interface) fraud is a type of cybercrime where scammers trick users into transferring money through fake payment requests, phishing links, or impersonation. It exploits users’ lack of awareness about how digital payment systems work.

Typically, the fraud begins when the scammer contacts the victim via phone call, SMS, or social media, pretending to be a bank official, customer support agent, or even a buyer/seller. Step 1: The scammer builds trust and creates urgency (e.g., account blocked, refund pending). Step 2: They send a “collect request” or a malicious link. Step 3: The victim is instructed to enter their UPI PIN or approve the request, often being told it is required to receive money. Step 4: Once approved, money is instantly deducted from the victim’s account and transferred to the scammer.

The most common targets include students, elderly individuals, and people new to digital payments, as they may not fully understand how UPI transactions work.

The consequences of such fraud include immediate financial loss, mental stress, and reduced trust in online payment systems. In many cases, recovering the lost money is difficult due to the speed of transactions.

This case highlights the need for awareness. Users should never approve unknown requests, share OTPs or PINs, and must always verify the authenticity of any payment-related communication.
