## 🛡️ Stay Safe Online Checklist (For College Students)

### ✅ Prevention Tips
- [ ] Use strong, unique passwords for all accounts and enable two-factor authentication (2FA)  
- [ ] Never share OTPs, passwords, or personal details with anyone—even if they claim to be from a bank  
- [ ] Avoid clicking on suspicious links or downloading unknown attachments  
- [ ] Keep your apps, devices, and antivirus software updated regularly  
- [ ] Check website URLs carefully (look for https:// and correct spelling) before entering details  
- [ ] Be mindful of what you post on social media—avoid sharing sensitive personal information  
- [ ] Log out from public or shared devices after use  

### 💰 UPI / Financial Safety Tips
- [ ] Never approve unknown “collect requests” on UPI apps—money is **debited**, not received  
- [ ] Do not scan random QR codes or click payment links from unknown sources  

---

### 🚨 In Case of Cyber Fraud
- 🌐 Report immediately at: https://cybercrime.gov.in  
- 📞 Call Cyber Crime Helpline: **1930**  

> ⏳ Act quickly! Reporting within the first few hours increases chances of recovering your money.
