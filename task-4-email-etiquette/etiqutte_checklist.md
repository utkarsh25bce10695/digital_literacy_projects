## ✅ Responsible Social Media Use Checklist

### ✔️ Do's
- [ ] Think before posting anything online  
- [ ] Keep your profile professional and respectful  
- [ ] Verify information before sharing  
- [ ] Protect your personal information and privacy  
- [ ] Use social media for learning and networking  

---

### ❌ Don'ts
- [ ] Don’t share fake news or unverified content  
- [ ] Don’t post offensive or inappropriate content  
- [ ] Don’t overshare personal details (location, phone, etc.)  
- [ ] Don’t engage in online arguments or negativity  
- [ ] Don’t waste excessive time scrolling aimlessly
